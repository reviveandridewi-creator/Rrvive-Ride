Services & Booking FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Service Area & Setup

Do I need to provide water or power at my location?

No, our mobile unit is completely self-contained with onboard water and electricity. All we require is a safe area with at least 3 feet of working clearance around the vehicle.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Wausau and provide mobile detailing within a 15-mile service radius.

How do you handle severe weather conditions?

To maintain safety and ensure high-grade results, inclement weather like heavy rain or freezing temperatures may require us to reschedule. We will reach out promptly to transfer your appointment to the earliest convenient date.

Booking, Fees & Cancellations

Is a deposit required to reserve an appointment?

Yes, a 25% non-refundable deposit is collected upon booking to lock in your time slot. This deposit is applied directly toward your service total on the day of your detail.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We request reasonable notice for changes. Cancellations or rescheduling requests made within 48 hours of your scheduled start time will incur a fee equal to 35% of the total service cost.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, along with all major credit and debit cards processed on-site. The remaining balance is due immediately upon completion of your service.

Vehicle Preparation & Safety Policies

How should I prepare my vehicle prior to arrival?

Please clear all personal belongings and remove child safety seats before our technician arrives. Revive & Ride Detailing is not responsible for moving, storing, or replacing personal property left inside the vehicle.

How are pre-existing damages handled?

Our cleaning process often uncovers underlying defects. We conduct an initial visual assessment and are not liable for pre-existing scratches, clear coat failure, paint chips, or material wear revealed during cleaning.
