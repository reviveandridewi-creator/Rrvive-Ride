Revive & Ride Detailing — Terms of Service & Client Policies

1. Booking, Deposits & Cancellations

  • Booking Deposit: A 25% non-refundable deposit is required at the time of booking to secure your appointment slot. This amount is applied directly toward your final service total.
  • Notice Window: We request at least 24 hours' notice for any appointment rescheduling.
  • Cancellation Policy: Deposits are strictly non-refundable. If you cancel your appointment at any time, the 25% deposit is retained to compensate for the reserved time slot and lost scheduling opportunities.

2. Mobile Service & Site Requirements

  • Fully Self-Contained Unit: Revive & Ride Detailing is completely independent. We do not require access to your home or office's water spigot or electrical outlets; we bring everything needed to complete the job on-site.
  • Working Space: Please ensure the vehicle is parked in a safe, legal location with adequate clearance to work around all doors and the trunk (minimum 3 feet of open space on all sides). We reserve the right to refuse service if the vehicle is parked in an unsafe or restricted location.

3. Weather & Rescheduling

  • Inclement Weather: High-quality detailing requires favorable conditions. In the event of heavy rain, severe storms, or freezing temperatures, we reserve the right to reschedule your appointment for the safety of our technicians and the quality of the finish.

4. Payment Terms

  • Final Payment: The remaining balance is due in full immediately upon completion of the detailing service, prior to the technician's departure.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: All major credit and debit cards processed securely via Square.

  • Pre-Existing Damage: Revive & Ride Detailing is not responsible for pre-existing paint defects, clear coat failure, dents, scratches, or interior wear that may be hidden beneath dirt, grime, or personal items and only become visible after the cleaning process.
  • Child Safety Seats & Personal Valuables: Clients are strongly encouraged to remove all personal belongings and child safety seats prior to service. If any car seats or personal items are handled or moved, you agree that Revive & Ride Detailing assumes zero liability for their proper reinstallation, structural integrity, or any loss/damage.
  • Deposit & Cancellation Terms: All bookings require a 25% deposit to secure your appointment slot. Deposits are strictly non-refundable; cancelling your appointment at any time will result in the forfeiture of your deposit.
